## Make yourself at home

Create your character, tend your garden, fish by the shore and get to know the villagers.
When adventure calls, choose one of eight heroes and explore a story across ten chapters,
with new places, dungeons and bosses along the way.

## Bring your friends

- **Around one screen:** join with a phone, gamepad or keyboard. Scan a QR code and your phone becomes a controller.
- **From your own home:** open an invitation link to see the game on your screen and play together. The host keeps the game running.
- **Come back later:** use **Continue** to return to your saved solo or multiplayer adventure.
